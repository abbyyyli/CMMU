    1. EQ: recognize and manage

    Emotional intelligence (EQ) 情緒智商 is the ability to assess emotions in oneself and others, and to manage oneself and relationships.

    Recognition

    Notice the emotion, its trigger and the other person's view.

    Management

    Choose a response that fixes the issue, keeps the standard and protects the relationship.

    EQ comes from nature and nurture: genetics, life experience and training. It can be developed.

    EQ origins: Goleman, Working with Emotional Intelligence (1999), cited on lecture p.4.

    Study example: correcting an inaccurate report. The sequence links the five factors in one conversation.

    中文邏輯情緒是訊號，行為是選擇。保留績效標準，同時改變處理問題的方法。

    3. Self-awareness and self-control

    Self-awareness 自我覺察

    Accurate knowledge of your emotions, preferences, typical reactions and core values, including what happens when you compromise them.

    Self-control 自我調節

    Feeling positive and negative emotions without letting them drive unproductive conduct.

    Notice the trigger→Name the feeling→Choose a response→Review the impact

    A person can recognize anger accurately and still respond badly. Awareness without control is not enough.

    S1 personality preferences show default habits and blind spots. They do not measure EQ. A Thinking preference does not explain cruelty, and a Judging preference does not mean being judgmental.

    4. Empathy with honesty

    The lecturer balances empathy and compassion with honesty. Understanding a concern does not mean dropping the standard. A good response covers both the person and the work.

    中文邏輯同理是理解處境，誠實是說清楚標準。兩者可以同時做到，不需要二選一。

    7. Develop EQ in the workplace

    Lecturer's methodWhat it means
    Coaching, feedback and supportA coach or mentor gives regular guidance
    Honest assessmentTrusted people say how your behavior affects them
    Personal planPick a few target behaviors; use work as the learning lab
    MeditationOne technique to build self-awareness
    Specific incident→Trusted feedback→Target behavior→Workplace practice→Observe and revise

    Applies to both roles. A leader practices receiving criticism without retaliation. A follower practices disagreeing respectfully and asking for a clear standard.

    Measure at the right level

    LevelExample measure
    ActivityCoaching sessions attended
    BehaviorRespectful, private corrective conversations
    Employee experienceStaff feel safe raising concerns
    Business outcomeHigh-performer turnover, customer response, product launches

    Better results at one level do not prove change at another. Attending workshops is not changed behavior.

    9. Do not mix these up

    Awareness and control are two of the five factors.

    They are taught separately but counted together as Factor 1. Awareness identifies the emotion; control governs the response.

    EQ means hiding anger.

    EQ recognizes positive and negative emotions and manages the response. Feeling frustrated and giving respectful correction can coexist.

    Empathy means agreeing or dropping the standard.

    Empathy understands another perspective. Acknowledge the difficulty, ask about support and still require accurate work.

    Empathy and social expertise are interchangeable.

    Empathy understands how another person sees the situation. Social expertise handles the interaction, including conflict, support and relationships.

    Compliance proves personal influence.

    Authority can secure obedience. Personal influence inspires willing support, especially when work needs initiative or cross-team cooperation.

    A profit target is a shared vision.

    A target specifies a result. Vision connects that result to purpose and values; employees need to understand the broader direction.

    T or J explains poor EQ.

    T emphasizes decision criteria; J favors structure and closure. Neither measures EQ or explains public humiliation.

    Completing coaching proves EQ improved.

    Attendance is an activity measure. Check repeated behavior, employee experience and business results separately.

    10. Cold call practice

    Define EQ and name its five factors in order.

    EQ assesses emotions in self and others and manages self and relationships. The factors are self-awareness and self-control, empathy, social expertise, personal influence, and mastery of vision.

    What distinguishes awareness from control?

    Awareness recognizes feelings, triggers, values and patterns. Control governs the behavioral response to those feelings.

    How does empathy differ from social expertise?

    Empathy understands another perspective. Social expertise uses that understanding to handle interactions, conflict, support and relationships.

    What does nature plus nurture mean here?

    Genetics, experience and training all shape EQ, so it can be developed.

    What makes vision more than a target?

    It connects priorities and actions to purpose and values, giving direction through obstacles. A numeric target can sit inside that direction.

    What does the lecturer recommend for development?

    Coaching, feedback and support; honest assessment from trusted people; a personal plan with work as the learning lab; meditation for self-awareness.
