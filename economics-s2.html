    Elasticity and Its Applications

    Session 1 showed which direction price and quantity move. Elasticity 彈性 shows how much they move, and decides whether a market change shows up mainly in price, in quantity, or in revenue.

    Demand Supply Revenue gained Revenue lost

    Chapter map

    Price changes→How easily can buyers or sellers adjust?→Elastic or inelastic→Revenue effect→When a curve shifts: mostly P or mostly Q?

    一句話Law of demand 告訴你方向；elasticity 告訴你幅度。

    1. Price elasticity of demand

    Price elasticity of demand (PED) measures how responsive quantity demanded is to a change in price.

    Elastic 富彈性

    Quantity changes by a larger percentage than price. Buyers switch, cut back, delay or cancel.

    Inelastic 缺乏彈性

    Quantity changes by a smaller percentage than price. Buyers keep buying: few substitutes, urgent need, small budget share.

    PED = % change in quantity demanded ÷ % change in price
    |PED| > 1 elastic · |PED| < 1 inelastic · |PED| = 1 unit elastic

    The sign is usually negative because price and quantity move in opposite directions. Classify by the absolute value: PED = −2 is elastic.

    Both curves slope down, so the direction is the same. Elasticity is about responsiveness, not direction.

    2. What makes demand more elastic?

    One question covers all five factors: if the price rises, how easily can consumers change their behavior?

    FactorMore elastic whenExample
    Substitutes 替代品More close substitutesOne chocolate brand versus chocolate as a whole
    Market definitionThe market is defined more narrowlyTangerines versus fruit
    NecessityThe purchase is discretionaryPremium snacks versus essential medicine
    Time horizonConsumers have longer to adjustFuel: short run you still drive; long run you buy an EV or move closer
    Budget share 占預算比重The purchase is a large share of incomeRent ฿20,000 → ฿24,000 versus snack ฿30 → ฿33

    These factors push elasticity in a direction. In a real case, say which one matters most and why.

    Market definition: why narrower means more elastic

    A narrower market has more substitutes outside its boundary. If tangerines get expensive, buyers switch to oranges or apples. If all fruit gets expensive, there is little to switch to.

    Manhattan rentals

    Renters can move to Brooklyn or Queens, so demand is more elastic.

    All NYC rentals

    Leaving means another city or a long commute, so demand is less elastic.

    注意只看一棟大樓不代表沒有替代品，附近相似的大樓都是替代品。個人「只想住 Manhattan」是個人偏好，不等於市場彈性。

    Elasticity depends on context

    Ride-hailing at 1 a.m. in heavy rain

    Few alternatives, urgent need: relatively inelastic.

    Ride-hailing to a café on Sunday

    Walk, take the train, go later or stay home: relatively elastic.

    Elasticity is not a permanent label on a product. It depends on substitutes, urgency, time and the consumer.

    3. Elasticity and total revenue

    Total revenue = P × Q. A price change creates a price effect and a quantity effect; elasticity tells you which one is stronger.

    DemandPrice risesPrice falls
    ElasticRevenue fallsRevenue rises
    InelasticRevenue risesRevenue falls
    Unit elasticRoughly unchangedRoughly unchanged

    Revenue is not profit. Profit also depends on cost, competition, regulation and reputation. "Demand is inelastic, so raise the price" is an incomplete recommendation.

    • Essential medicine: likely inelastic (necessity, few substitutes), so a price rise may raise revenue. Ethics, regulation and affordability still limit the decision.
    • One brand of premium snack: likely elastic (many substitutes, easy to skip). A strong category does not give one brand pricing power.

    4. Elasticity of supply

    How responsive is quantity supplied to a price change? Ask: how easily can production rise at low cost?

    Elastic supply

    Spare capacity, available inputs, fast production, easy entry. Toothpicks.

    Inelastic supply

    Fixed capacity, scarce resources, legal or natural limits, long production. Original Picasso paintings, durian trees that take years to fruit.

    Class slide: the same price rise from $40 to $50 moves quantity supplied from 80 to 170 when supply is elastic, but only from 80 to 85 when it is inelastic.

    Supply, like demand, becomes more elastic in the long run: firms build capacity, hire, and new firms enter.

    Demand elasticitySupply elasticity
    Whose behaviorConsumersProducers
    Core questionHow easily can buyers switch, cut back or delay?How easily can producers expand output?
    Over timeMore elasticMore elastic

    5. When a curve shifts, which moves more?

    The elasticity of the curve that does not move decides whether the adjustment shows up in price or in quantity.

    SituationPriceQuantityClass case
    Demand shifts, supply elasticSmall changeLarge changeFood Waste (flat supply)
    Demand shifts, supply inelasticLarge changeSmall changeFood Waste (steep supply), Housing
    Supply shifts, demand inelasticLarge changeSmall changeWar on Drugs, Onionomics
    Demand rises, supply perfectly elasticNo changeLarge changeGun buyback

    6. The cases

    Ending food waste (class slides)

    Less waste→Market demand for food falls→P and Q fall→Size depends on supply elasticity

    With inelastic supply, price falls a lot, which helps poor food buyers most. But many poor people are food farmers, whose income falls. A full answer covers both groups.

    War on Drugs: methamphetamine (class slides)

    Enforcement shifts supply left. Because demand is inelastic, price rises sharply while quantity falls only a little. Total spending, and seller revenue, can rise. "Quantity fell, so the policy worked" is incomplete.

    Onionomics (assigned reading, BBC)

    Onions are a staple in India with few substitutes, so demand is very inelastic. Bad weather, storage losses or transport limits cut supply, and prices spike while quantity barely falls. In a bumper harvest the reverse happens: prices collapse and farmers lose. The price rise came from supply falling, not demand rising.

    Slave redemption (MRU)

    Humanitarian groups buying enslaved people's freedom add demand. If supply is very inelastic, price rises but few additional people are supplied, so the program frees people who would otherwise stay enslaved. If supply is elastic, the higher price draws in more supply and can encourage more captures. Good intentions do not remove supply incentives.

    Housing (MRU)

    More jobs raise housing demand. Where land and building rules make supply inelastic, as in San Francisco, the demand increase shows up mostly as higher prices.

    Gun buyback (MRU)

    A local area is linked to a much larger market, so supply is close to perfectly elastic. Guns bought back are replaced from outside at the same price, so the number of guns in use barely changes.

    7. Common mistakes

    The law of demand and elasticity are the same thing.

    One gives direction, the other gives magnitude.

    Necessities are always inelastic.

    Substitutes, time and budget share also matter.

    A more specific product is more inelastic.

    A narrower market usually has more outside substitutes.

    Inelastic demand plus a price rise means higher profit.

    It means higher revenue. Profit depends on cost.

    Inelastic demand means quantity never changes.

    Quantity changes, just relatively little.

    Onion prices rose, so demand increased.

    Supply fell; demand did not need to move.

    Inelastic supply means supply shifted left.

    Elasticity is the curve's shape. A shift is a separate event.

    Ending food waste helps all poor people.

    Poor buyers gain; poor farmers who sell food lose.

    8. Cold call practice

    What is elasticity?

    How responsive quantity is to a change in price.

    Law of demand versus elasticity?

    The law of demand gives the direction; elasticity gives the size of the change.

    What makes demand more elastic?

    More close substitutes, a narrower market, a discretionary good, more time to adjust, and a larger budget share.

    Why is demand for tangerines more elastic than for fruit?

    Tangerine buyers can switch to many other fruits; there is little to switch to from fruit as a whole.

    How does elasticity affect total revenue?

    With inelastic demand a price rise raises revenue; with elastic demand it lowers revenue.

    Does higher revenue mean higher profit?

    No. Profit also depends on costs.

    What makes supply inelastic?

    Fixed capacity, scarce resources, legal or natural limits, or a long production process.

    When demand shifts, what decides whether P or Q changes more?

    The elasticity of supply. Inelastic supply means mostly price; elastic supply means mostly quantity.

    Why do farmers sometimes earn less in a good harvest?

    Food demand is inelastic, so a large supply increase causes a big price fall and revenue drops.

    Why are housing prices so high in some cities?

    Land limits and building rules make supply inelastic, so rising demand mostly raises price.
